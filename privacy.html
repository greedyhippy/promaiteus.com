Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Early-stage website notice. Promaiteus is currently operating a simple “coming soon” website. This notice explains how we handle personal data in connection with that website and pre-launch enquiries.

1. Who we are

Promaiteus is an AI training and automation business operating in Ireland. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Promaiteus is the controller of personal data covered by this notice.

2. Information we collect

The current website is a static informational page. It does not provide user accounts, online payments, contact forms or newsletter sign-up.

We may receive personal information when you choose to contact us through an email address, social-media profile or other contact channel that we make available. This may include your name, contact details, organisation, message contents and any information you choose to provide.

Our hosting and security providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and security logs.

3. Why we use information

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, discuss potential services, maintain business records, operate and secure the website, and meet legal obligations. Where required, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and protecting our business, taking steps requested before entering into a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, or your consent.

4. Cookies and analytics

The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Essential technical logs may be created by hosting, content-delivery and security providers to deliver the site and prevent abuse. If we introduce non-essential cookies or analytics, we will update this notice and provide any consent mechanism required by law.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We use service providers that help us host, secure and operate our website and communications. They may process personal data only as needed to provide those services or comply with law. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where this occurs, we will use an appropriate transfer mechanism where required.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to an enquiry, maintaining records, resolving disputes and meeting legal, accounting or reporting obligations.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of or objection to processing, and portability of data you have provided to us. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

8. Contact and changes

To exercise your rights or ask a privacy question, please contact Promaiteus through the contact details published on our website or the channel through which you contacted us. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and appropriate.

We may update this notice as our website and services develop. The latest version will always be published on this page.
